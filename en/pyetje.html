Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All the material is open, with no ads and no sign-up. We don't even ask for an email.

I don't know a single Arabic letter. Can I start?

The site was built exactly for you. Start with the alphabet and go one letter a day, there is no deadline.

How long does it take to read the short surahs?

With 15 minutes a day, most people read the short surahs within two to three months. Fluency comes later, with repetition.

Does this site replace a teacher?

No. Correct pronunciation is best learned by listening and being corrected by a person. The site prepares you and keeps you on track between lessons.

Which languages is the site in?

Albanian, English, Dutch and German. New material comes out in Albanian first, then in the other languages.

Which Quran translations do you use?

The Arabic text is from Tanzil (Uthmani, Hafs). The English meaning is Sahih International. In Albanian we use Hasan Efendi Nahi, in the revised edition by QuranEnc, in Dutch Sofian S. Siregar and in German Abu Rida Muhammad Ibn Ahmad Ibn Rassoul.

Where does the religious content come from?

We do not make anything up ourselves. The explanations on the Miracles and Reflection pages come from IslamQA, and each part links to the answer. The lessons about the Prophet ﷺ come from the book "Interactions of the Greatest Leader". If you find a mistake, please tell us.
