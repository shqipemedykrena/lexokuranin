The Miracles of the Quran

The Quran was revealed more than 1400 years ago. Here are some of its signs and signs of the Prophet ﷺ, explained simply. Each part links to its source.

The Book itselfNews that came trueMiracles of the Prophet ﷺSigns in creationNumbers in the Quran

The Book itself

A Book that has not changed

Allah promised that He Himself would preserve the Quran, both its words and its meaning, so that nothing is added to it or taken away.

The Companions learned it by heart and wrote it on palm stalks and thin white stones. After the battle of al-Yamamah, where many who knew it by heart died, Abu Bakr asked Zayd ibn Thabit to collect it in one place. A verse was only accepted when two Companions testified that they had heard it from the Prophet ﷺ.

Since then the Quran has passed from generation to generation, in people's hearts and in writing. Not one letter of it has changed.1, 2, 3

The challenge no one won

Allah challenged mankind and the jinn in stages: to bring a book like the Quran, then ten surahs, then just one surah.

The challenge includes even the shortest surah, and it still stands today. More than 1400 years have passed and no one has met it, although people had both the motive and the chance.

The message of the Prophet ﷺ is for all people until the Day of Resurrection. That is why his greatest miracle, the one that remains forever, is the Quran itself.3, 4

A Book without contradictions

Allah says that if the Quran had been from anyone other than Him, people would have found much contradiction in it. This is a proof that the Quran is from Allah.

Al-Qurtubi counts it among the miracles of the Quran: everything it mentions is in harmony, without any contradiction. When someone thinks there is a contradiction, this comes from reading without reflection, and the contradiction "is only in his mind".

Many differences that look like contradictions are differences of situation, time or character. For example, the Quran says man was created from water, from dust, from clay and from sounding clay. These are stages, not contradictions.5, 6, 4

A Prophet who could not read or write

The Quran calls Muhammad ﷺ the Prophet who can neither read nor write, as he was described in the Torah and the Gospel (Quran 7:157).

Before the Quran he did not read any book and did not write anything with his own hand. When revelation came, his scribes wrote it down.

This removes any suspicion that he learned the message from earlier books. Yet he told of past events and answered the questions of the People of the Book. This is evidence of the truth of his prophethood.7, 4

News from the unseen

The Quran proves its divine origin by telling the stories of the Prophets and past nations. After the story of Noah, Allah says that this is news of the unseen, which neither the Prophet ﷺ nor his people knew before.

About Maryam it says: "you were not with them when they cast lots with their pens" (Quran 3:44), and likewise about Musa at the mountain and in Madyan (Quran 28:44-45).

An-Nahhas says these stories were a proof for Quraysh, because the Prophet ﷺ did not know how to write, did not mix with the People of the Book, and none of them lived in Makkah.8, 4, 9

News that came true

The news about the Romans

The Persians defeated the Romans "in the nearest land". The idol worshippers of Makkah rejoiced and mocked the Muslims.

They said: "Those who have no Book have defeated those who have a Book," and they told the Muslims they would defeat them too, just as the Persians had defeated the Romans. To Quraysh the Persians were far stronger. They said: "The Romans cannot defeat the Persians, because the Persians are stronger than they are."

The Quran said that the Romans would win "within a few years". The Arabic word bid' means three to nine years. Abu Bakr made a bet with Quraysh for seven years. When seven years had passed, the Prophet ﷺ told him: "Add two years." Before those two years ended, the news came that the Romans had defeated the Persians.

According to Ibn al-Qayyim, this Roman victory came in the year of Hudaybiyyah (6 AH). When Allah gave him victory over the Persians, Heraclius, the king of the Romans, walked from Homs to Iliya (Jerusalem) to give thanks.

Al-Zajjaj says this is proof that the Quran is from Allah, because no one else could have known it.10, 11, 12

The Prophet's ﷺ news that came true

The Prophet ﷺ never foretold anything that would happen in the future without it happening exactly as he said.

He said that a fire would come out in the Hijaz and light up the necks of the camels in Busra (Bukhari 7118, Muslim 2902, from Abu Hurayrah). This fire appeared in the year 654 AH, about 644 years after his death.

He said that none of those alive that night would live more than a hundred years. The last Companion, Abu'l-Tufayl, died in 110 AH, and this is one of the signs of prophethood. He also foretold that 'Ammar would be killed by "the transgressing group", and 'Ammar was killed at Siffin (Bukhari 447, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri).13, 14, 15, 16

Miracles of the Prophet ﷺ

The splitting of the Moon

The people of Makkah asked for a sign, and they were shown the Moon split into two parts. It is narrated by Anas ibn Malik (Bukhari 3868, Muslim 2802).

Ibn Kathir mentions that Mount Hira was seen between the two parts: one behind the mountain and the other in front of it. It happened at full moon.

This is reported in many hadiths by many narrators, and the Muslims are unanimously agreed on it. It was a miracle of the Prophet ﷺ that ended in his lifetime, while the Quran remains. Science not confirming it does not mean it did not happen.17, 16, 18

Water flowing from his ﷺ fingers

Among the miracles of the Prophet ﷺ is the water springing from between his fingers. The water increased so much that the whole army drank from it and made wudu.

'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud said: "I saw the water springing from between the fingers of the Messenger of Allah" (Bukhari 3579). In the same report he says they used to hear the food glorifying Allah as it was eaten.

Such miracles were given to the Prophet ﷺ as an honour and to increase the faith of his Companions.16, 13, 17

The palm trunk that cried

On Fridays the Prophet ﷺ used to give the sermon leaning on a palm trunk. When the minbar was made and he moved to it, the trunk cried.

The trunk groaned like the lowing of a bull, in the report of Anas ibn Malik (ad-Darimi 41). The Prophet ﷺ came down and embraced it, and it fell silent. He said that had he not embraced it, it would have kept groaning until the Day of Resurrection.

In the report of Jabir ibn 'Abdullah (Bukhari) the trunk cried like a small child, because of the remembrance of Allah it used to hear. This is one of the miracles of the Prophet ﷺ.19, 20, 16

The food that increased

Among the miracles of the Prophet ﷺ is the increase of small amounts of food in his hands, so that the whole army ate and there was still food left over.

At the Battle of the Trench, Jabir's family had only one sa' of barley and one lamb. The Prophet ﷺ invited the people of the trench. There were one thousand men. They ate until they were full and left, while the pot was still brimming as before and the dough was still being baked (Muslim 2039).

The bread of Abu Talhah and Umm Sulaym also fed about seventy or eighty men, who came in groups of ten.21, 16, 13

The stone that greeted him

The Prophet ﷺ said that in Makkah there was a stone that used to greet him with salaam before his mission began (Muslim 2277, from Jabir ibn Samurah).

This is one of his miracles, together with the pebbles that glorified Allah in his hand.22, 16, 17

Signs in creation

Before you start

The greatest miracle of the Quran is its own words: its language, style and guidance. Scholars warn against tying the Quran to scientific theories that change, and against spreading unverified "miracles". So for the signs in creation we only show what our sources state.27, 28, 29

The creation of man, stage by stage

The Quran describes that man is not created all at once but in stages. Researchers confirmed this only about 60 years ago.

The stages, in the order of verses 23:12-14: nutfah in "a safe lodging", which is the womb; then 'alaqah, a clot; then mudghah, a little lump of flesh; then bones; then the bones are clothed with flesh; then "another creation".

The embryologist Professor Marshall Johnson, when shown these verses, said: "There is no explanation except that this is revelation from above!"3, 23

The barrier between two waters

Allah released the two waters: one sweet and palatable, the other salty and bitter, and placed a barrier between them. Most commentators say these are the fresh-water rivers and the salty sea.

According to Ibn Kathir, the barrier is the dry land between rivers and seas. According to Ibn Abbas and al-Qurtubi, it is an unseen barrier that Allah placed where a river meets the sea, so the waters do not mix. Ibn Ashur describes it as a barrier caused by the difference in density between the two waters, which oceanographers speak of today.

These views do not contradict each other and both can be accepted. It is one of the signs of creation that point to the Creator.24, 25

Darkness in the deep sea

The Quran describes darkness in a deep sea, covered by waves upon waves, darkness upon darkness.

The oceanographer Professor Dorjaro explains: divers once could not go deeper than about 20 metres, and at about 200 metres there is intense darkness. The darkness comes in layers, because the colours of light disappear one after another: red first, blue last.

"Waves upon waves" is explained as a separation between the upper and lower layers of the sea, with waves inside the sea itself. Dorjaro concluded that this cannot be human knowledge.3

Mountains as pegs

Allah set the mountains firmly so that they stabilise the earth and keep it from shaking.

With a tent peg, the part driven into the ground is longer than the part above it. The roots of mountains go several times deeper below the surface than the part that rises above it, and geological research shows the mountains are firmly fixed and stabilise the earth.

Mountains move a few millimetres a year, but this does not contradict their being described as stable.26

Numbers in the Quran

Before you start

"Numerical miracles" are not a proof of the Quran. The counts are often wrong or chosen to reach the result people want. The real miracle is that no one can bring anything like the Quran. So here we do not present the numbers as proof, but as an honest count.30, 31
