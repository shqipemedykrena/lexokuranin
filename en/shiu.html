Rain and water, signs of Allah

Rain falls, the earth comes alive and we drink water. This page is about rain as one of the signs of Allah and about what the Prophet ﷺ did when it rained. We have summed it up in six steps, in simple words.

Scroll down: the rain falls on the village

Step 1

Rain, a mercy from Allah

Allah sends the winds as good news before His mercy, the rain, and sends down pure water from the sky. Beneficial water is among Allah's creations, together with minerals, trees and animals, a proof of the greatness and power of the Creator.

Allah asks about the water we drink: is it you who bring it down from the clouds, or are We? If He willed, He could make it salty. Why then do you not give thanks? (Quran 56:68-70). These verses show that Allah provides for His creation and that only He deserves worship.2

Step 2

From water, every living thing

Among the clear verses that show this universe has a Creator is this verse: from water Allah made every living thing.

Allah sends down water from the sky (Quran 6:99, 27:60). And He asks: is it you who make it grow, or are We the Grower? (Quran 56:64).3

Step 3

The dead earth comes alive

Anyone with insight can see the earth dry and dead, with no plants. When the rain falls on it, it flourishes and its plants come to life after being dead. The One Who brings the earth back to life after its death is able to bring the dead back to life (see also Quran 7:57).

Allah will make the bodies grow from the earth by means of rain. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Then Allah will send down water from the sky and the dead bodies will grow as plants grow" (Bukhari 4651, Muslim 2955, from Abu Hurayrah).4, 3, 5

Step 4

Say: we got rain by the bounty of Allah

The hadith from Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani is found in Bukhari and Muslim. Whoever says "We got rain by the bounty and mercy of Allah" believes in Allah and disbelieves in the stars. Whoever gives the credit to a star disbelieves in Allah and believes in the stars.

Linking rain to a star is a kind of disbelief. Believing that stars have power of their own is major shirk. Even saying it without that belief is still a serious wrong.6

Step 5

What the Prophet ﷺ did when it rained

When it rains, it is recommended to make du'a, like the one 'Aishah narrated (Bukhari 1032). It is also recommended to let the rain fall on you. The Prophet ﷺ lifted part of his garment so the rain would fall on him and said: "Because it has just come from its Lord, may He be glorified and exalted" (Muslim 898, from Anas).

When the rain became too heavy, he ﷺ said: "O Allah, around us and not upon us" (Bukhari 1014).

Du'a at the time of rain is one of two du'as that are not rejected. The other is du'a at the time of the call to prayer (al-Hakim 2534, at-Tabarani 5756, from Sahl ibn Sa'd).

Allahumma sayyiban nafi'an

O Allah, make it a beneficial downpour.1

Bukhari 1032, from 'Aishah

Step 6

Stop and reflect

This verse mentions the rain that Allah sends down from the sky to give life to the earth after its death, and the winds and clouds between the sky and the earth. These are signs for people who use reason.

According to as-Sa'di, these signs show the oneness, power and mercy of the Creator. Praiseworthy reflection is when the heart is humbled before the Lord of the Worlds and the person then acts on what they have reflected on.7

And Allah knows best.
