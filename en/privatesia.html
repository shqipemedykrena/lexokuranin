Privacy

Read the Quran has no accounts, asks for no email and shows no ads. We do not collect data about you.

Your progress is saved only in this browser, on your device (localStorage). Nothing is sent to us. If you use another device or a private window, your progress is not there.

What is saved in your browser

  • The verses you know by heart
  • The surahs you have read and how many times you finished the whole Quran
  • Your saved verses and where you stopped reading
  • The letters you know and how often you practised them
  • Reading settings: text size, translation and night mode
  • The language you chose and your answer to the first question
  • Tonight's checklist before sleeping (it starts again every day)

The fonts of the site load from Google Fonts, and some pages load the 3D library three.js from cdnjs. When they load, these services can see the IP address of your browser.
