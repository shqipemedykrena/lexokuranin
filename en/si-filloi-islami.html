How did Islam begin?

Islam is not a new religion. It is the same message all the prophets brought: worship Allah alone, the Creator. With Prophet Muhammad ﷺ this message was completed.

The same message from the beginning

Islam is the religion of all the Prophets: they all called people to worship Allah alone. The word "Islam" means submission to Allah.1

How it spread, step by step

  1. around 608 or 609

    The first word: Read

    In the fortieth year of his life, the first revelation came to the Prophet ﷺ in the cave of Hira. He went home with his heart pounding. Khadijah reassured him, and she was the first woman to believe in him.2, 3, 4

  2. the first 3 years · around 609–612

    The first believers

    For about three years after the first revelation, he called people to Allah in secret. Among the first to believe were Khadijah, Abu Bakr and Ali.5, 4, 6

  3. then · around 612

    The open call

    Allah commanded him to proclaim the message openly. The leaders of Quraysh rejected his call to worship One God instead of their many gods. They offered him wealth and kingship to stop, but he refused.5, 3, 7

  4. the years in Makkah · around 609–622

    Hard years

    Believers such as Bilal, Ammar and his mother Sumayyah were tortured. Bilal had no tribe to protect him. Some Muslims, at the Prophet's ﷺ command, migrated to Abyssinia, where the king, the Negus, let them stay in his land.8, 7, 9

  5. the Hijrah · 622

    The move to Madinah

    The Prophet ﷺ migrated to Madinah. He built the mosque of Quba and then his own mosque, made brotherhood between the emigrants and the Ansar, and made a written peace treaty to defend Madinah.3, 10, 11

  6. 23 years · 609–632

    The Quran comes down slowly

    The Quran was revealed piece by piece over twenty-three years, according to events. The Companions memorised it, and it was written down in front of the Prophet ﷺ by scribes he appointed.12, 13, 14

  7. 8 AH · 630

    Makkah without revenge

    In 8 AH he conquered Makkah and pardoned its people, saying: "Go, for you are free." Then people entered the religion of Allah in crowds, as Surah an-Nasr describes.3, 15, 16

  8. 10 AH · 632

    The religion was completed

    In 10 AH, during the Farewell Pilgrimage, the verse "This day I have perfected your religion for you" (Quran 5:3) was revealed at Arafah. After his death Islam spread east and west, and later reached Europe.3, 17, 5
