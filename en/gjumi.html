Sleep, a sign of Allah

Every night we sleep and every morning we wake up. This page is about sleep as one of the signs of Allah and about how the Prophet ﷺ slept. We have summed it up in six steps, in simple words.

Scroll down: the night passes in the village

Step 1

Sleep is one of His signs

Allah mentions sleep among His signs, together with seeking His bounty. He made the night a covering and sleep a rest (Quran 25:47, 78:9).

Sleep is a blessing from Allah that gives a person back their strength after tiredness.2

Step 2

Night for rest, day for living

Allah made the night for us to rest and the day for us to work and earn a living (Quran 27:86).

So we usually sleep at night and are active by day, although sleeping in the daytime is not forbidden.2

12:00

Step 3

Sleep, a small death

The Quran and the Sunnah show that Allah takes the souls during sleep (see also Quran 6:60). Ibn Rajab says the verse shows that sleep is a kind of death.

The Prophet ﷺ said to his Companions who had slept: "Allah took your souls when He willed, and He returned them when He willed" (Bukhari 7474, from Abu Qatadah).

Yet, according to Ibn Rajab, the soul is not cut off from the body completely as in death.1

Step 4

Before sleeping, like the Prophet ﷺ

The Prophet ﷺ said: "When you go to bed, do wudu as for prayer, then lie down on your right side" (Bukhari 239, from al-Bara ibn 'Azib).

He ﷺ slept on his right side, with his right hand under his right cheek. Sleeping on the left side is allowed, but the reward of following the Sunnah is missed.

Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya

In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.3, 4, 5

Bukhari 6324, from Hudhayfah

Step 5

What he recited before sleeping

Every night he ﷺ put his palms together, blew into them and recited al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq and an-Nas. Then he wiped over as much of his body as he could, starting with his head and face, three times (Bukhari 5017, from 'Aishah).

Whoever recites Ayat al-Kursi when going to bed is protected by Allah until morning (Bukhari 2311, from Abu Hurayrah). Reciting the last two verses of al-Baqarah every night is also Sunnah (Bukhari 5009, Muslim 808).

Step 6

Waking up with praise

When he woke up, the Prophet ﷺ praised Allah for giving him life back after taking it. This shows that sleep is a kind of death.

After waking he used the miswak and recited the last ten verses of Surah Al Imran.

Al-hamdu lillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur

Praise be to Allah Who gave us life after He caused us to die, and to Him is the resurrection.1, 4

Bukhari 6312, from Hudhayfah
