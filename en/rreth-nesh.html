About us

Read the Quran is a free site for learning to read and understand the Quran, in Albanian, English, Dutch and German. The name "Read the Quran" comes from the Arabic word "iqra", which means "read", the first word of the revelation.

No ads, no sign-up and no email. Your progress is saved only in your browser.

Where our sources come from

  • The Arabic text of the Quran: Tanzil (Uthmani, Hafs).
  • Translations: Hasan Efendi Nahi for Albanian (revised edition by QuranEnc), Sahih International for English, Sofian S. Siregar for Dutch and Abu Rida Muhammad Ibn Ahmad Ibn Rassoul for German.
  • The explanations on Miracles, Reflections, the Names of Allah and the Glossary: IslamQA (islamqa.info), with a link to each answer.
  • The lessons about the Prophet ﷺ: the book "Interactions of the Greatest Leader".
  • Photos of the Earth: NASA. The Milky Way: ESO/S. Brunier (CC BY 4.0).

Our rule

We do not make anything up ourselves. If the source does not say something, we do not write it. If you find a mistake, please tell us.
