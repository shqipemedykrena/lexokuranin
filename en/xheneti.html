Paradise, the reward of the believers

Allah has prepared Paradise for the believers. This page is about Paradise, its degrees and the way to enter it. We have summed it up in six steps, in simple words.

al-Firdaws Degree ar-Rayyan

Scroll down: climb degree by degree

Step 1

Real, already created, and beyond imagination

Paradise and Hell are created things that exist now. Paradise is prepared for the pious, and the word "prepared" shows that it exists now (Quran 3:133). Paradise is real, not a symbol, and believing in it is obligatory.

Of what Allah has kept for those highest in status, the Prophet ﷺ said: no eye has seen it, no ear has heard it, and it has never entered the heart of man (Muslim 189, from al-Mughirah ibn Shu'bah). So the real bliss of Paradise is beyond our imagination.

Even the lowest-ranked person in Paradise will have the equal of the whole world and ten times as much (Bukhari 6202, Muslim 186, from Ibn Mas'ud). A space in Paradise the size of a whip is better than this world and everything in it.2, 3, 1, 4

Step 2

Gardens, rivers, shade and a pure drink

Paradise has gardens underneath which rivers flow, two gardens with spreading branches and fruit in pairs, and flowing springs. Its provision is eternal and so is its shade, and its provision will never finish.

From al-Firdaws spring the four rivers of Paradise (Ahmad 22738, at-Tirmidhi 2531, from 'Ubadah ibn as-Samit). Al-Kawthar is a river in Paradise (Quran 108:1; at-Tirmidhi 3284, from Ibn 'Umar). Its banks are domes of pearls and its water is whiter than milk and sweeter than honey.

The wine of Paradise is white and delicious. It causes no intoxication, no loss of reason, no headache and no harm, unlike the wine of this world (Quran 37:45-47).5, 6, 1, 7, 8

Step 3

No fatigue, always young, forever

There no fatigue will touch them and they will never be asked to leave. They will not want to move anywhere else (Quran 18:107-108). There is whatever the souls desire and whatever delights the eyes (Quran 43:69-71).

Allah removes resentment, envy and rancour from their hearts, so they are brothers whose hearts are as one (Bukhari 3006, Muslim 5063, from Abu Hurayrah). They will be in the most beautiful form, the form of their father Adam, aged thirty or thirty-three. Their youth will never end and their garments will never wear out (at-Tirmidhi 2462, from Abu Hurayrah).9, 10, 11

Step 4

One hundred degrees, and al-Firdaws at the top

There is one Paradise, but it has degrees and levels. Paradise has one hundred degrees, and the distance between each two is like the distance between heaven and earth (Bukhari 2637, from Abu Hurayrah). Al-Firdaws is the highest, best and middle part of Paradise, and above it is the Throne of the Most Gracious (Ahmad 22738, at-Tirmidhi 2531, from 'Ubadah ibn as-Samit).

The people of Paradise will see those in the highest places above them as you see a brilliant star far away on the horizon, because of the difference in their status (Bukhari 3256, Muslim 2831, from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri). The Prophet ﷺ taught: when you ask Allah, ask Him for al-Firdaws. The believer should aim for the highest place.12, 1, 13, 14

1 / 100

Step 5

The gates, and the way in

Submission to Allah is the criterion for entering Paradise: believers who do righteous deeds, stay steadfast and sincerely repent, men and women alike. Righteous deeds are the cause, but no one enters Paradise by his deeds alone. Entry is by the grace and mercy of Allah, while ranks in Paradise vary according to deeds.

In Paradise there is a gate called ar-Rayyan, through which those who fast will enter (Bukhari 1896, Muslim 1152, from Sahl ibn Sa'd). Paradise has many gates, such as the gate of prayer, of jihad and of charity, and each person is called from the gate of the deeds he was known for (Bukhari 1897, Muslim 2027, from Abu Hurayrah).

Doing wudu well, walking to the mosque and waiting for one prayer after another raise a person in status and erase sins (Muslim 251, from Abu Hurayrah).15, 16, 17, 12

Step 6

The greatest blessing: seeing Allah

Seeing Allah is not possible in this life, but it will happen after death. Because it is the greatest blessing, it is kept for Paradise, where only the believers will receive it.

When the Companions asked whether they would see their Lord, the Prophet ﷺ compared it to seeing the sun and the full moon on a cloudless day: you will see Him just as clearly (Bukhari 6088, from Abu Hurayrah). This delay of the vision is itself a reason to do good deeds in this world.18

And Allah knows best.
