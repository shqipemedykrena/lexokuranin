Who is your Lord?

Allah has beautiful names. When you know them, you love Him more, fear Him and hope in Him. Here are 103 of His names with their meanings, following the book by Dr. Abdur-Razzaaq al-Badr on the beautiful names of Allah. Tap a name for the explanation.

Ninety-nine names

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one. Whoever learns them will enter Paradise."8, 2

What does learning them mean?

  1. Memorising them.
  2. Understanding their meanings.
  3. Acting on them, for example believing that Allah is One and seeking provision only from Him.
  4. Calling on Allah by them, for example: "O Most Merciful, have mercy on me!"3, 4

Are there only 99?

No. Scholars agree that the names of Allah are not limited to 99. The hadith is about entering Paradise, not about a fixed number. The Prophet ﷺ said in a du'a: "or that You have kept in the knowledge of the unseen with You", which means Allah has names that no created being knows.2

How to benefit

  • Knowing Allah through His names, attributes and deeds is the noblest knowledge, says Ibn al-Qayyim.
  • This knowledge brings love, fear, hope and sincerity towards Allah, and increases faith.
  • Call on Allah by His names, as the Quran says in 7:180: "O Ghafoor, forgive me!", "O Tawwaab, accept my repentance!"
  • It is not enough just to repeat the names without understanding them and acting on them. And there is no basis for using a name as a cure for a disease.5, 6, 4, 7
